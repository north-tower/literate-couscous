Getting started

An admin creates your account. You sign in, connect your own LinkedIn, then send the first campaign when Chrome is free.

Only one campaign uses Chrome. If someone else is already sending, yours waits until theirs finishes.

  1. 1

    Get your invite

    There is no public sign-up. An admin creates you as a Campaign user and sends the email and temporary password in a private message.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Use that email and password on the login page. You land on your own campaign. Your people list stays on your account.

  3. 3

    Connect your LinkedIn

    Enter your own LinkedIn username and password. Sendline stores the password encrypted and does not share it. On later visits, leave the password blank to keep the one already saved.

  4. 4

    Write the campaign

    Add one full name per line, or download the CSV template and upload it. The message must include {name}, filled with each person’s first name. An attachment is optional and must be under 25 MB.

  5. 5

    Launch the first time while Chrome is free

    Chrome opens on your profile. If LinkedIn asks you to sign in or complete two-factor, do that in the automation window. Later runs reuse that session.

One Chrome for everyone

If another client is already sending, your launch waits in line and starts when theirs finishes. The page shows “Waiting — another campaign is using Chrome.” You also cannot start a second campaign of your own until the first one ends. Stop closes only your run.
