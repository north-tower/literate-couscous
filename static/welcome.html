Onboarding

Five steps to your first campaign

Everything below is the same flow your admin expects you to follow after invite.

Only one campaign uses Chrome at a time. If someone else is sending, yours waits in line until theirs finishes.

  1. 1

    Get your invite

    An admin creates you as a Campaign user and sends email plus a temporary password privately.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Use that email and password on the login page. Your people list stays on your account.

  3. 3

    Connect LinkedIn

    Enter your LinkedIn login once — stored encrypted. Leave password blank on later visits to keep the saved one.

  4. 4

    Write the campaign

    One full name per line or upload the CSV template. Message must include {name}. Attachment optional, under 25 MB.

  5. 5

    Launch while Chrome is free

    Chrome opens on your profile. Complete LinkedIn sign-in or 2FA in that window once; later runs reuse the session. Watch the live log and stop anytime.

One Chrome for everyone

If another client is already sending, your launch waits in line. The UI shows when Chrome is busy. You cannot start a second campaign of your own until the first ends — Stop only closes your run.
